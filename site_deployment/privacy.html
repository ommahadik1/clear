Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 23, 2026

1. Zero Data Collection

clear. was built from the ground up with absolute contextual privacy in mind. We believe your data is yours alone. Therefore, our extension operates with a strict Zero Tracking policy. We do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize any of your personal data, browsing history, keystrokes, or screen contents.

2. 100% Local Processing

All masking, blurring, and content manipulation features provided by the extension are processed entirely on your local machine using hardware-accelerated algorithms. No information ever leaves your device or is communicated to external servers.

3. Cookies and Analytics

We do not use cookies, trackers, or any third-party analytics within the extension. Our landing page may use basic local storage exclusively to remember your light/dark mode preference, but this information is never transmitted to us or any third party.

4. Compliance with GDPR and CCPA

Because we do not collect any personal data, we are inherently compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and other global privacy frameworks. There is no personal data for us to provide, rectify, or erase.

5. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Because we do not collect contact information, we cannot notify you directly of changes. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the extension constitutes acceptance of the latest policy.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our GitHub repository issues page.
